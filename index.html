Active Institutional Deployment

Pawar Public School, Dombivli

CISCE Affiliated (ICSE / ISC) • Maharashtra • 100+ Teaching Faculty • Grades 1 to 12

The Challenge

Coordinating morning coverage across 100+ teaching staff and multi-grid timetables without schedule collisions or burdening the same cooperative teachers.

The Implementation

An on-premise administrative desktop deployment that evaluates real-time faculty availability and enforces an automated 2-duty daily ceiling.

The Outcome

Double-booking eliminated, workload transparently distributed, and verified staffroom substitution notices ready before morning assembly.

Administrative Comparison

Administrative Control vs Manual Coordination

A clear comparison between traditional paper cross-referencing and structured digital substitution management.

Without S.W.A.P.

Manual Administrative Friction

  • Manual Timetable Cross-Checking Coordinators search through paper binders and spreadsheets during the morning rush.
  • Risk of Schedule Collisions High risk of accidentally assigning teachers with lab, zero period, or library commitments.
  • Unequal Duty Distribution Without workload tracking, the same available teachers are repeatedly assigned substitutions.
  • Hurried Staffroom Communication Handwritten slips lead to missed classes, delayed periods, and unattended classrooms.
With S.W.A.P.

Structured Management

  • Instant Timetable-Aware Availability Queries master schedules in milliseconds to identify every genuinely free teacher.
  • Automated Conflict Validation Strict deterministic checks guarantee zero double-booking across multi-grid setups.
  • Enforced Workload Guardrails Automatically enforces a maximum 2-duty daily ceiling, sorting candidates equitably.
  • Printable Staffroom Rosters Generates formatted physical substitution slips for the staffroom noticeboard in one click.
