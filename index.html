The Administrative Reality

Managing teacher absences shouldn't mean managing chaos.

Every morning, school coordinators face the same high-pressure routine. When teachers are absent, keeping classrooms covered requires reconciling multiple schedules before the morning assembly.

01

Unexpected Faculty Absences

Unplanned sick leaves and emergency notices arrive minutes before the school day starts, triggering an immediate administrative scramble.

02

Manual Schedule Reconciliation

Coordinators must cross-reference paper master grids, departmental sheets and spreadsheet tabs to find who is genuinely free for each period.

03

Unequal Substitution Burden

Without centralized tracking, the same few cooperative teachers repeatedly receive substitute periods, leading to faculty burnout.

04

Fragmented Daily Records

Substitutions scribbled on whiteboards or paper chits lack an audit trail when disputes, administrative queries or workload reviews arise.

05

Multi-Grid Timetable Clashes

Schools running combined primary, middle and senior grades face conflicting period timings, zero periods and split electives that increase errors.

Result

Lost Instructional Time

Administrative energy is drained by routine scheduling logistics instead of academic leadership and student well-being.

The Solution

One organised workflow for teacher substitutions.

SWAP replaces manual guesswork with a deterministic, structured system. It analyzes your school’s complete timetable database to produce conflict-free substitution schedules in minutes.

Faster substitution planning

Select absent teachers and instantly review coverage requirements for the day. No paper flip charts or manual cross-referencing.

Better visibility of faculty availability

Know exactly who is teaching, who is free, and who has already been assigned a substitution period across every hour of every weekday.

Organised timetable management

Centralize faculty and class timetables from Grade 1 to Grade 12 into a single reliable database with versioned upload history.

Easier monitoring of assignments

Generate printable daily substitution sheets for staffroom bulletin boards and maintain a permanent digital record of every substitution run.

Feature A

Substitution Management

Mark absent teachers and generate substitution schedules using the existing workflow. The engine suggests conflict-free teachers who are genuinely free for that specific period, while allowing the coordinator full manual override control.

  • Searchable absent faculty dropdown with immediate slot resolution.
  • Preview coverage options showing all available free teachers per period.
  • One-click confirmation with printable substitution notice sheets.
Engine Rule

Deterministic conflict check

Zero double-booking guarantee across all active grades.
Feature B

Timetable Management

Upload and maintain faculty and student class-wise timetable data. The system supports structured timetable imports across primary and secondary grades, preserving period numbers, subject names, and room allocations.

  • Supports master faculty timetable and class section grids.
  • Upload history audit trail with filenames, upload dates, and processing status.
  • Grade 1 through Grade 12 unified period mapping.
Data Storage

Unified Timetable Slots

Single source of truth linking teacher, class, period and day.
Feature C

Faculty Availability

Visualise free periods, regular classes and assigned substitutions. The availability view highlights truly free periods in green, busy scheduled classes in gray, and newly assigned substitution duties in distinct red markers.

  • Teacher-wise cards with total free period counts.
  • Period-by-period matrix displaying every free educator per bell.
  • Day selection (Monday–Friday) with optional date-specific duty lookups.
Visual Status

Green / Gray / Red

Free period, regular class duty, and active substitution duty.
Feature D

Substitution History

Review previously generated substitution records with full date-stamped logs. Administrators can inspect past runs, verify which teacher covered which lecture, and maintain accountability across academic terms.

  • Chronological record of saved substitution runs.
  • Auditable logs of absent teachers and allocated substitutes.
  • Persistent local records accessible anytime without cloud dependency.
Audit Trail

Permanent Run Logs

Verifiable history for administrative reviews and inspections.
Feature E

Workload Visibility

Help administrators understand faculty availability and substitution allocation. Built-in logic balances substitute assignments across available teachers and prevents overloading any single individual beyond administrative limits.

  • Fair allocation prioritizing teachers with fewer cover duties.
  • Daily cap protection (maximum 2 substitution duties per teacher per day).
  • Clear visibility into total teaching loads across departments.

Balanced Distribution

Simple Process

How SWAP works in everyday school operations.

A simple, four-step routine designed to fit seamlessly into morning administrative preparations.

Step 1

Upload Timetables

Import your faculty and class-wise timetable data into the school's local SWAP installation at the start of the term.

Done once or when timetables update
Step 2

Mark Absences

Each morning, select the faculty members who are absent through a quick searchable dropdown.

Takes ~30 seconds in the morning
Step 3

Generate Substitutions

Review conflict-free substitution suggestions, adjust manual assignments if desired, and confirm the schedule.

Automated conflict checking
Step 4

Monitor Availability

Export the printable substitution sheet for staff noticeboards and review updated availability rosters.

Ready before first bell rings
