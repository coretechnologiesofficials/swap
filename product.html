Core Modules

Comprehensive Administrative Toolkit

Each module is tailored to address specific operational bottlenecks in daily school scheduling.

Module 01

Teacher Substitution Management

What It Does

Identifies all scheduled lectures affected by reported teacher absences and presents eligible substitute candidates for every vacant period.

How It Operates

Cross-references absent teachers with the day's master slots, isolates vacant classes, and checks all active teachers against scheduled duties, zero periods, and daily duty counts before suggesting conflict-free candidates.

Administrative Benefit Reduces morning coordination from hours of paper cross-checking to under two minutes.
Module 02

Faculty Timetable Management

What It Does

Maintains a centralized, searchable repository of all teacher master timetables across departments and grades.

How It Operates

Parses term-start timetable documents into structured records accommodating multi-grid streams (ICSE, ISC, and secondary sections), zero periods, and faculty non-teaching duty blocks.

Administrative Benefit Eliminates lost binders, conflicting spreadsheets, and fragmented departmental schedules.
Module 03

Class Timetable Management

What It Does

Maps section-wise class timetables from Grade 1 through Grade 12 (including sections 1A to 12E).

How It Operates

Stores room numbers, subjects, and assigned teachers for every period of the weekly bell schedule, enabling instant lookup by class section whenever student schedules need review.

Administrative Benefit Guarantees that no class section is left unattended due to untracked room or timetable shifts.
Module 04

Faculty Availability & Free Periods

What It Does

Instantly calculates and displays which teachers are free during any given period of any day of the week.

How It Operates

Offers dual viewing modes: an all-teacher matrix highlighting free periods and total free counts, and a period-by-period breakdown listing every candidate with zero scheduled teaching commitments.

Administrative Benefit Gives coordinators instant visibility of unassigned faculty without manual ledger cross-referencing.
Module 05

Workload Balancing & Guardrails

What It Does

Distributes substitution duties equitably across available faculty while enforcing an automatic daily ceiling.

How It Operates

Orders candidate suggestions by lowest cumulative substitution count today and filters out any teacher who has reached 2 daily duties, shielding cooperative faculty from repeated burden.

Administrative Benefit Protects teacher morale, prevents burnout, and ensures transparent duty distribution.
Module 06

Substitution Records & Auditing

What It Does

Chronologically archives every confirmed daily substitution run for institutional record-keeping and audit compliance.

How It Operates

Locks confirmed substitution rosters with exact dates, absent teachers, assigned substitutes, and class periods in a permanent local database searchable by date range.

Administrative Benefit Provides verifiable duty records for academic reviews, substitute allowances, and parent inquiries.

Designed for Every School Stakeholder

How S.W.A.P. provides operational control, transparent records, and fairness across leadership and faculty.

Leadership

For Principals

  • Institutional Continuity: Confidence that every period is reliably covered every morning.
  • Administrative Oversight: Complete operational visibility into daily faculty absences and coverage.
  • Audit-Ready Records: Permanent date-stamped logs for board reports and compliance checks.
Mechanism: Immutable local database logs prevent unrecorded substitutions or dispute ambiguities.
Academic Administration

For Vice Principals

  • Workload Monitoring: Objective data on cumulative substitution distribution across departments.
  • Multi-Grid Coordination: Synchronizes ICSE, ISC, and primary schedules without cross-stream friction.
  • Conflict Prevention: Strict algorithmic validation catches overlapping non-teaching assignments.
Mechanism: Unified query engine evaluates teacher commitments across all stream timetables simultaneously.
Daily Operations

For Coordinators

  • Instant Availability: Zero manual ledger searching during the stressful 7:30 AM morning rush.
  • 1-Click Override: Discretion to confirm candidate suggestion or reassign with a single click.
  • Formatted Notices: One-click printable noticeboard slips ready before morning assembly.
Mechanism: Automated candidate sorting presents the best free candidate while leaving ultimate authority with the coordinator.
Teaching Staff

For Teachers
